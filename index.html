The 200-Ton Precision Injection Moulding Cycle

Scroll through to observe the synchronized mechanical stages of our high-pressure moulding cycle, from raw granule plasticization to micro-metrology quality release.

BARREL TEMP 300°C

STEP 01 OF 05

Virgin Polymer Dehumidification & Heating

Granules enter the Sunsun 300°C pre-heater hopper to remove all trace moisture, eliminating hydrolysis and safeguarding high tensile strength in Delrin and Nylon 6 GF30%.

STEP 02 OF 05

200-Ton Hydraulic Toggle Clamp Lock

The 510 × 510 mm platen locks with 2,000 kN clamping force, providing absolute zero-deflection seal across all parting lines to eliminate component flash.

STEP 03 OF 05

High-Velocity Cavity Injection

The 50mm screw injects molten resin at up to 185 MPa through balanced runner gates, packing complex gear involutes and thin rib sections without internal voids.

STEP 04 OF 05

Conformal Thermal Crystallization

Dual-channel temperature regulation cools the polymer at calibrated rates, locking in amorphous toughness and semicrystalline rigidity with sub-micron shrinkage control.

STEP 05 OF 05

Demoulding & Mitutoyo QA Verification

Parts are ejected smoothly without pin-marks and transferred directly to our QA bench for Mitutoyo vernier dimensional audit and Shore hardness testing.

Founded in 2025 in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, Negizhi Industries bridges precision polymer science with industrial manufacturing rigor to eliminate product failure under extreme operating environments.

THE NEGIZHI METHODOLOGY

Value Engineering Without Compromising on Quality

Our engineering team works closely with customers from part design through mould flow simulation, tooling fabrication, and mass production. By replacing expensive, heavy metal components with specialized engineering polymers (Delrin, 30% Glass-Filled Polyamide, PPS), we reduce component weight by up to 70%, eliminate corrosion, and lower unit manufacturing costs.

  • Full-Fledged Tool Room: Die design, machining, precision grinding, wire cutting, and EDM sparking.
  • Electrical R&D: Formulations tailored for high dielectric breakdown and arc-track resistance.
  • Mechanical Testing Lab: Mitutoyo calipers, Shore hardness testing, bore gauges, and gram-level shot consistency.
100% Virgin Engineering Grade Polymers (DuPont, BASF, Sabic standard) with zero uncertified regrind
